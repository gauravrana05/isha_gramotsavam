## 🎯 **How Volunteers Will Use It**

1. **See Help Button**: Orange help button appears in bottom-right corner
2. **Get Contextual Help**: Questions relevant to current page shown first
3. **Search for Help**: Type keywords like "check in", "verify", "upload"
4. **Read Answers**: Click questions to see detailed answers
5. **Contact Support**: Escalate complex issues to human support
